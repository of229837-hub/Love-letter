A LETTER FROM MY HEART

To My Beautiful Queen

୨୧ ───── ♡ ───── ୨୧

Oh, my beautiful Queen,

I love you with all my heart. I just want to say that I am grateful every day for your little actions and all the things you do for us and for me. I wanted to take a moment to write you this letter and remind you how lucky and happy I am to have you.

Even on the days when you're a stubborn little baby or when things don't always go as planned, you're still my little Shayla, the one I adore every single day. And I want to keep adoring you for the rest of my life.

No matter how hard things get, how exhausted we become, or how impossible things may seem, I will always try my best to make things work for us. Because when it comes to us, I never want to give up.

I know we love each other so much, and I know you love me even more. I feel so grateful and lucky to receive that kind of love and treatment from you. Of course, I will never take it for granted.

I will keep trying to love you the way you want to be loved. I will try my best to make sure you never have to feel scared or uncertain around me. I want you to feel safe with me. I want to become your comfort space, the person you can always come to whenever life feels heavy or your heart feels low.

That's all from me, my love. And I hope that whenever I do something wrong or make you feel a certain way, you will always feel comfortable talking to me. I want us to communicate, understand each other, and grow together.

All I want is to make you happy and see that beautiful smile of yours. I will keep loving you with all my heart and make sure you continue to feel as lucky and loved as you did when our story first began.

Forever yours,

Heng ♡

My love, my happiness, my favorite person.
